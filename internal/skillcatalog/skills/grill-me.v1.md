identity: skill.grill-me
version: 1
name: Grill Me
description: Clarify consequential research choices before execution when the goal, scope, evidence boundary, or deliverable is genuinely ambiguous.
---
Use this Skill only before the Research Plan is accepted.

Identify the smallest unresolved choices that would materially change the investigation or report. Prefer one concise question at a time. Ask about the intended decision, audience, scope, exclusions, time boundary, named subjects, acceptable evidence, or report structure only when the answer changes the plan.

Do not turn a clear request into an interview. Do not repeat information the Member already supplied. Summarize accepted answers into the Research Plan. Once the Plan is accepted, stop asking ordinary clarification questions; route around source and tool failures using other evidence and disclose any remaining gaps in the Report.
